# Computational Macroeconomics: MATLAB and Dynare

This repository contains numerical implementations of dynamic macroeconomic models developed as part of the graduate course *Advanced Macroeconomics II* at Universidad EAFIT.

The exercises focus on solving and simulating macroeconomic models, analyzing their dynamics, and examining responses to economic shocks.

## Models

1. **Labor Market Search Model:** Numerical solution of a job search model with reservation wages and parameter sensitivity analysis.
2. **Capital Adjustment Costs:** General equilibrium model examining the effects of productivity shocks under different capital adjustment costs.
3. **New Keynesian DSGE Model:** Analysis of productivity and monetary policy shocks under alternative monetary policy regimes.
4. **Financial Accelerator (BGG) Model:** Analysis of financial frictions and the transmission of productivity and entrepreneurial net-worth shocks.

## Tools

- **Software:** MATLAB and Dynare.

## Academic Context

Graduate coursework in Advanced Macroeconomics II, Master's in Economics, Universidad EAFIT (2024).
